# Zomb Survive

Browser base-builder / wall-defense game. Open `index.html` in a browser (no build step).

- Draw free-form walls and towers (walls cost materials). Zombies path around gaps, so you can funnel them. Buildings act as walls.
- Defenders stand on walls/towers, shoot at range (uses ammo), and stab zombies that climb over (free).
- Everything sealed in with your core (up to a level-based limit) is your territory. Buildings inside it produce ammo, materials and food. Upgrade the core to take over more of the town.
- Nights bring escalating hordes of weak zombies, with runners and brutes mixed in later.

Controls: WASD/arrows or right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom, left-click/drag to build, 1-4 tools, U upgrade, N call the night, Space pause.

Tuning constants are at the top of `game.js`.
