ZOMB SURVIVE

The town is overrun. Your core is the last safe spot. Wall in the streets, claim the buildings inside your perimeter and use what they produce to grow.

  • Walls are free-form. Zombies path around them through any gap, so you can funnel them. Buildings also act as walls.
  • Defenders stand on walls and towers. They shoot at range (uses ammo), then stab zombies climbing over (free).
  • Territory is everything sealed in with your core, up to a limit. Upgrade the core to hold more of the town.
  • Night brings the hordes. They are weak, but there are a lot of them.
