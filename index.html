ZOMB SURVIVE

You are the only one left at this camp. The town is full of the dead, and there is no day or night to tell you when the next horde is coming.

  • Scavenge buildings (hold E) for materials, food and ammo. Some hide survivors.
  • Scout the fog. You only see what you or your lookouts can see, and noise (gunfire) draws zombies in.
  • Fortify the camp with walls and towers. Zombies never break walls. They pile up and climb over, so kill them as they climb.
  • Build lookouts to spot hordes before they arrive. Without them you get almost no warning.
  • A sealed camp attracts refugees. Post survivors on the walls: they shoot, then stab.
